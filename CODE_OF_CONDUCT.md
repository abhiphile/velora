# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Be considerate, constructive, and respectful of differing viewpoints. Accept feedback gracefully. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers listed by the repository hosting organization.
